# Privacy Policy — RealEstate Agent Bot

**Last updated: 9 October 2026**

RealEstate Agent Bot is an Instagram messaging and real estate enquiry service in development. It helps participating real estate agents collect and organise property preferences shared by people who contact their Instagram account.

## Information we may collect

When you message or interact with a participating Instagram account, the service may receive the information made available through Meta's Instagram APIs, including:

- Your Instagram username, account identifier and messages sent to the participating account.
- Information you choose to share about your property search, such as suburb, budget, property type, bedrooms, bathrooms, parking and intended timeframe.
- Relevant comments and interactions that you use to start an enquiry.
- Technical information required to deliver, troubleshoot and secure the service, including webhook events and timestamps.

Please do not provide passwords, banking details or sensitive identity documents in a property enquiry.

## How we use information

We may use information to respond to enquiries, ask follow-up questions, organise customer property requirements, show enquiries to the real estate agent or business you contacted, and identify potentially relevant properties. We also use limited technical information to test and maintain the service.

The service is currently being tested and not all features described above are available yet.

## Sharing

Enquiry information may be accessible to the participating real estate agent or business that you contacted and authorised personnel operating the service. Instagram and Meta also process information under their own policies. We do not sell personal information to data brokers or advertisers.

## Retention and security

Information may be stored in application databases or technical logs for as long as reasonably needed for the enquiry, testing, service operation, security and applicable legal requirements. We aim to limit access to those who need it. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices and data deletion

You can choose not to provide further information. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information held by RealEstate Agent Bot, contact **vahid.mehrasa@yahoo.com** with the subject **RealEstate Agent Bot — Privacy Request**. Please identify the Instagram account involved and the nature of your request. Do not send passwords or access tokens. We may need to verify that the request comes from the relevant account before processing it.

Deleting service records does not necessarily delete separate records held by Meta or a participating real estate business. You can use Instagram's own settings for data held by Instagram.

## Updates

We may update this policy as the service develops. The updated version will be posted here with a new date.
